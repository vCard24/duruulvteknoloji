Why Autumn Is a Critical Window

Cooling nights and crop transitions create opportunities for pests to establish overwintering populations. Treating now protects next season’s start.

Why Pest Pressure Rises in Autumn

Harvest gaps, plant debris, and milder days let aphids, thrips, and whiteflies rebound if monitoring loosens after peak summer work.

Advantages of Autumn Applications

Lower canopy density after harvest can improve spray access. Reducing base populations cuts spring chemical load.

Role of Greenhouse ULV Systems in Autumn

ULV fills airspaces quickly with less water—useful for empty or transitional houses. See greenhouse ULV, Entosis 20, and Sera Max 50.

Pre-Winter Checklist

Clean residues, map hot spots, schedule ULV passes, service machines, and stock formulations. Plan next orders via the quote form.
