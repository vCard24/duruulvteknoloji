Are Window Screens Enough for Fly Control?

Screens are an excellent physical barrier, yet they do not kill breeding populations outdoors or insects already indoors. Integrated control pairs exclusion with source treatment and, when needed, ULV.

What Screens Truly Solve: Blocking Entry

Quality mesh on windows and doors cuts night mosquito intrusion and daytime flies when frames seal well and doors are not left ajar.

What Screens Cannot Solve: Pests Already Inside or in the Yard

Garden breeding sites, trash, and damp corners rebuild numbers. Screens alone leave those reservoirs untouched.

Why Use Both Together

Exclusion reduces re-entry after outdoor adulticiding; ULV or professional treatment reduces yard and room pressure. Handheld options live under handheld ULV.

Choosing the Right Screen (Kayseri Context)

Match screen type to window/door hardware—pleated, hinged, or sliding. Local fitters help with measurements; pair that with equipment advice via the quote form.
